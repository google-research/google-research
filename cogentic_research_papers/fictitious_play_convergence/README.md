## Abstract

How slowly can simultaneous discrete-time Fictitious Play (FP) converge in a
finite two-player zero-sum game of size N? While Robinson (1951) and Shapiro
(1958) established an O(T^{-1/(N-2)}) upper bound on the empirical duality gap,
Karlin (1959) conjectured a universal O(T^{-1/2}) rate. Daskalakis and Pan
(2014) refuted the strong form of Karlin's conjecture by showing that when
best-response ties are broken adversarially at each round, simultaneous FP on
the n × n identity matrix I_n can converge at the slow rate Ω(T^{-1/n});
however, their lower bound relies crucially on adversarial tie-breaking, and
Abernethy, Lai, and Wibisono (2021) proved that fixed lexicographic tie-breaking
restores the O(T^{-1/2}) rate on I_n and all diagonal games with positive
diagonal entries. Recently, Wang (2025) refuted the weak form of Karlin's
conjecture (under any tie-breaking rule) by constructing a 10 × 10 game (N = 20)
that couples three copies of Rock-Paper-Scissors to achieve a duality gap of
Ω(T^{-1/3}). However, whether tie-free simultaneous FP can converge at rates
slower than Ω(T^{-1/3}), and how the convergence exponent α(N) in Ω(T^{-α(N)})
scales with the game size N, remained open; in particular, iterating a
three-copy block construction across d levels would require exponential game
size N_d = Ω(3^d).

In this paper, we resolve the polynomial dimension dependence problem for
simultaneous FP under fixed lexicographic tie-breaking. For every integer d ≥ 1,
we construct an explicit rational skew-symmetric game A_d ∈ [-1, 1]^{n_d × n_d}
of quadratic size N_d = 2n_d = 4d^2 + 14d - 10 = Θ(d^2) (with N_1 = 8) such that
simultaneous FP initialized at (X_0, Y_0) = (0, 0) is strictly tie-free at every
round t ≥ 2 and satisfies Gap_{A_d}(x̄_T, ȳ_T) ≥ κ_d T^{-1/(d+1)} for all
T ≥ T_0(d), giving α_d = Θ(N_d^{-1/2}). Our key innovation is a *single-copy
run-and-reset gadget* that embeds a single copy of the level-(ℓ-1) game M_{ℓ-1}
into M_ℓ bordered by only m_ℓ = 4ℓ + 5 pure helper strategies, resetting the
inner cumulative utilities in place via division-free linear algebra in a
descending binomial polynomial basis. In concurrent and independent work,
Abernethy, Lazarsfeld, and Wibisono
([2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08768)) also obtain games with duality gap
Ω(T^{-α(N)}) for α(N) = Θ(N^{-1/2}).

## Concurrent Work

In concurrent and independent work, Jacob Abernethy, John Lazarsfeld, and Andre
Wibisono
([*Arbitrarily Slow Polynomial Convergence of Fictitious Play*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08768),
arXiv:2610.08768) also construct skew-symmetric games of quadratic size
N = Θ(d^2) in which, after both players play a prescribed common first action,
every subsequent best response is unique and simultaneous Fictitious Play has
duality gap Θ(T^{-1/(d+1)}), giving convergence exponent α(N) = Θ(N^{-1/2}).
